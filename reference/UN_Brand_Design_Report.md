# UN Global Compact — Official Design Rules Report

Extracted from `assets/2026_UN_Global_Compact_Brand_Guidelines.pdf` (61 pages, March 2026 edition). Full text: `reference/UN_Brand_Guidelines.txt`.

## 1. Brand Tone & Voice
- Characteristics: business-friendly, collaborative, clear & concise, aspirational, authoritative.
- Tone spectrum: Personal/Sociable (social media, blogs) → Instructional/Direct (press releases, toolkits, email, infographics) → Composed/Formal (UN correspondence, publications, reports, executive updates).
- Writing: simple, active voice, short sentences, no jargon, scannable structure, no double spaces.
- Naming rules: use "United Nations Global Compact" first, then "UN Global Compact"; avoid "UNGC", "Global Compact", possessive form, "10 Principles" (use "Ten Principles"), "Global Goals" preferred over "SDGs" for business audiences. Dates: Day/Month/Year. British "ou" spelling + American "z" spelling (Canadian English).

## 2. Logos
- Three logo levels: Masterbrand, Country Network, Endorser (participants only, by request).
- Masterbrand logo: globe with "UN GLOBAL COMPACT" arch above, olive branches below. Logotype combines globe + "United Nations Global Compact" in two fixed lines ("United Nations" Flama Medium, "Global Compact" Flama Light). Translation of lettering not permitted.
- Country Network logo: masterbrand + country name one line below olive branches (Flama Light). Network logotype: two lines "Global Compact" / "Network [Country]".
- Endorser logo: "WE SUPPORT" (Flama Medium, capitals) above masterbrand logo.
- Logos only in Global Compact Blue, black, or white. White only on solid colour or calm image area; blue is primary colourway.
- Clear space = ≥50% of globe width/height. Minimum digital size: 24 mm (masterbrand logo/logotype/network logotype); 18 mm (endorser). Minimum print size: 15 mm (masterbrand), 29 mm (endorser).
- Logos must not be altered; participants use only provided files.

## 3. Iconography
- Ten Principles icons: used on white in Global Compact Blue, or white on blue; secondary colours allowed for differentiation (e.g., social media). Issue colours: Human Rights #CCB146 (gold), Labour #6E417A (purple), Environment #297D6D (green), Anti-Corruption #EC3740 (red).
- SDG icons: use in entirety, never edit colours; SDG colour wheel/stripe must be shown complete. Never pair Global Goals with secondary palette — only primary blues.

## 4. Brand Palette
- Primary: Global Compact Blue PANTONE 540 U — #1E3250 (RGB 30,50,80; CMYK 94,80,42,38), plus tones: #4C6B8B, #699CC6, #AECFE6.
- Secondary (accent): Purple #6E417A, Green #297D6D, Gold #CCB146, Red #EC3740.
- Pairing: primary + Global Goals colours OK; primary + secondary OK; never all three palettes together; never pair secondary colours with Global Goals; avoid clashing secondary colours.

## 5. Typography
- Primary typefaces: Flama (main), Roboto (digital copy/website), Lora (print/highlighting quotes & subheadings). All weights allowed.
- Noto Sans for Arabic, Chinese, Georgian, Japanese, Korean, etc. Times New Roman for strictly legal documents. Verdana for slide decks if Flama unavailable.
- Rules: no extra font families, max three variations, no skewing/stretching/warping, no manual bolding of Flama Light, no excessive line lengths, no double spacing.

## 6. Imagery
- Original, authentic, unposed, high-quality photos; diverse and inclusive representation; respect subjects; avoid overediting; vibrant colours; proper attribution.
- Layouts: Patchwork (square tiles, 2 mm gap, sizes relative to data), Gradient (blue background, image right, gradient 60% of canvas, copy on gradient area), Simple (blue overlay 65% opacity + SDG bar; SDG bar on any side).

## 7. Templates
- PowerPoint: two cover options (full-bleed imagery / white footer for external logos).
- Video: lower thirds with ribbon, Flama only, soft fades/slides/wipes, music suited to mood.
- Email signature: Flama Book or sans-serif default, Global Compact Blue name & links, clear space around logo.
- Stationery: business card & letterhead; black or 80% grey body copy.
- Publications: impactful front cover using golden ratio; SDG banner horizontal ~2/3 down page.
- Print: A4/US Letter/Square, 3 mm bleed, CMYK, matte laminate cover, 280gsm cover / 180gsm internal, perfect bind or saddle stitch, FSC-certified paper.

## 8. Social Media
- Handles: X/Instagram/Threads/TikTok @globalcompact; Facebook @UNGlobalCompact; LinkedIn @united-nations-global-compact; YouTube @United Nations Global Compact.
- Hashtags: #MobilizingBusiness #UNGlobalCompact #TenPrinciples #ForwardFaster #GlobalGoals #Goal1 etc. (not #SDG). Logo solid blue or white only, min size & clear space. Dimensions: IG Feed 4:5 1080×1350; Stories/Reels 9:16 1080×1920; X/LinkedIn 16:9 1920×1080.

## 9. Official Boilerplate Text
- Full (107 words), medium (73 words), one-line (49 words) versions provided; full preferred. Ends with @globalcompact / unglobalcompact.org.

## 10. Translations
- Translations of publications accepted only if reviewed by the relevant Country Network, no additions/subtractions, one acknowledgment line. Official UN-language terms table provided (Arabic: "الأمم المتحدة", "الاتفاق العالمي للأمم المتحدة", "المبادئ العشر", etc.).

## 11. Sub-brands
- Leaders Summit: Flama Extrabold lockup, white or black; each year choose 3 SDG colours + black/white. Flag system: modular 3-colour letter abstractions.
- Forward Faster: separate initiative with its own toolkit.
- Flagship events: convene/inspire/educate/mobilize.

## Key Implications for the Magazine
- Replace current red-based palette with Global Compact Blue (#1E3250) as primary; use secondary accents sparingly.
- Typography: Flama/Roboto/Lora (not Amiri/Alexandria/Archivo). For Arabic text use Noto Sans.
- Logo: use official UN Global Compact globe logo (blue/black/white only) with clear space; no red logo.
- Imagery: authentic, diverse, properly attributed; consider patchwork/gradient/simple layouts.
- Publications: SDG banner horizontal ~2/3 down cover; golden-ratio composition.
- Naming/wording: "United Nations Global Compact", "Ten Principles", "Global Goals", boilerplate text on back cover.
